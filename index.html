🧬 Macromolecule Academy

Before analyzing evidence, detectives must learn how scientists identify macromolecules in food samples.

🍞 Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates provide quick energy.Foods include bread, pasta, rice,potatoes, and sugars.

  • Benedict's Test detects glucose.
  • Iodine Test detects starch.

🥩 Proteins

Proteins help build and repair cells.Foods include meat, eggs, beans,nuts, and dairy products.

Biuret Solution detects proteins.

🧈 Lipids

Lipids store energy and are found in oils, butter, fats, and many animal products.

Sudan III detects lipids.

🔬 Positive Test Results

  • Sudan III → Red Oil Layer
  • Biuret → Purple Solution
  • Benedict's → Orange/Red Solution after heating
  • Iodine → Blue-Black Solution
